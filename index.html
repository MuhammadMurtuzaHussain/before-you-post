Photo metadata check

Before you post.

Your phone writes more into a photo than the picture. Drop one in to see what it gives away, then download a clean copy. Nothing is uploaded: the file never leaves this tab.

    What gets removed

    Kept
    Every pixel, untouched. JPEGs are cleaned byte-for-byte with no re-compression. The colour profile and the "which way is up" flag stay so the photo looks the same.
    Removed
    GPS, timestamps, camera make, model and serial number, owner name, editing software, XMP and IPTC blocks, and hidden comments.
    Not covered
    What is in the picture. A street sign, a house number or a reflection still gives you away.

    Open source. No tracking, no server, works offline once loaded.
